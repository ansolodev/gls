1.0 Purpose & Scope

This framework establishes the institutional standards for the collection, storage, processing, and lifecycle management of student records at Greatland School[cite: 1]. Modeled after global academic data governance best practices, this policy ensures absolute alignment with the Philippine Data Privacy Act (DPA) of 2012 through the enforcement of three core pillars: Transparency, Legitimate Purpose, and Proportionality[cite: 1].

This policy applies universally to all academic registrars, administrative staff, IT operations personnel, and third-party contractors handling student information.

3.0 The Data Lifecycle & Retention Mandate

In alignment with world-class data minimization standards, Greatland School explicitly rejects indefinite data hoarding. Data is retained only for its specified institutional lifecycle[cite: 1]:

01 Intake / Collection[cite: 1]
02 Active Tracker[cite: 1]
03 Regulatory Archival[cite: 1]
04 Secure Purge[cite: 1]
  • Active Phase: Data remains active for the full duration of the student's enrollment[cite: 1].
  • Archival Phase: Core academic sheets (Form 137/SF10) are permanently archived per Department of Education regulations[cite: 1]. Secondary sensitive files (such as 4Ps qualification sheets) are transitioned into physical cold storage offline[cite: 1].
  • Destruction Phase: Obsolete paper entries containing sensitive information are permanently destroyed using Level P-4 cross-cut paper shredders[cite: 1]. Systemic backups are digitally purged using cryptographically secure wiping algorithms[cite: 1].

4.0 Physical & Administrative Security Protocols

To mitigate the risks associated with manual, paper-based enrollment workflows, the following operational mandates are strictly enforced across the campus registrar[cite: 1]:

🔒 The Zero-Exposure Rule: No completed enrollment forms may be left face-up or unattended on public-facing intake tables[cite: 1]. Forms must immediately be turned face down or fed into secure dropboxes upon counter receipt[cite: 1].

🔒 Access Separation: Hard-copy forms must be moved directly into dual-lock security cabinets accessible exclusively by authorized registry personnel[cite: 1].

🔒 Screen Privacy Obfuscation: Terminal screens running database software or updating the DepEd Learner Information System (LIS) must be equipped with physical polarizing micro-louvers (privacy filters) and must face entirely away from the student queuing line[cite: 1].

5.0 Data Subject Rights Enforcement

Greatland School guarantees parents, legal guardians, and authorized learners the full execution of their rights as defined by global privacy frameworks and the National Privacy Commission (NPC)[cite: 1]:

Right of Access

Request a secure, validated copy of all digital entries and historical paper metadata stored for your account[cite: 1].

Right to Rectification

Inaccurate or incomplete contact information will be corrected across all active databases within 48 business hours of written notice[cite: 1].

Right to Object

Revoke consent for optional institutional workflows (such as yearbook features or school marketing activities) at any point without impacting academic standing[cite: 1].
